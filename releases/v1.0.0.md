# v1.0.0 — 2026-02-28

## Overview

This is the first public release of the platform. It lays the foundation: a web console, organizations, repositories, AI agents, and team management.

## Added

- **Console.** A web console to manage the platform from one place.
- **Organizations.** Create organizations and work within their scope.
- **Repositories.** Connect and manage repositories inside an organization.
- **AI agents.** Create and configure AI agents that operate on your repositories.
- **Team.** Invite teammates and manage who is part of your organization.
